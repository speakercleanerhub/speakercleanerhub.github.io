1. What Our Tools Can and Cannot Do

The tools on this Site play sound tones that can help dislodge dust, lint and small amounts of water from a phone speaker by vibrating the speaker diaphragm. They are a cleaning aid, not a repair service.

Sound cleaning cannot fix hardware damage. It cannot repair a blown or torn speaker, a damaged diaphragm, a loose internal connection, corrosion from water damage, or any other physical fault. If your speaker is damaged, no sound played through it will restore it, and continuing to play loud tones through a damaged speaker could make the damage worse. If cleaning does not help, stop and take your phone to a qualified repair shop. See When to Visit a Repair Shop for Speaker Problems.
