Contact Us

We would love to hear from you. Whether you have a question about cleaning your phone speaker, found a mistake in one of our guides, or have an idea for a new tool or guide, send us an email and we will get back to you as soon as we can.

Email Us

Email: contact@speakercleanerhub.github.io

When you write to us, it helps if you include your phone model (for example, iPhone 13 or Samsung Galaxy A54) and a short description of the problem. That way we can give you a more useful answer.

What You Can Contact Us About

  • Questions: your speaker still sounds quiet or muffled after trying our tools and guides.
  • Corrections: you spotted an error in a guide or a tool that does not work as described.
  • Requests: a new guide or tool you would like us to add, such as a guide for a specific phone model.
  • Feedback: anything about the site that could be better, clearer or easier to use.

Before You Write

Many common problems are already covered in our guides. If your phone volume dropped suddenly, start with Phone Volume Suddenly Low? Fixes That Work. If your speaker crackles or distorts, read How to Fix a Crackling Phone Speaker. If you think the problem may be hardware damage, see When to Visit a Repair Shop for Speaker Problems.

Please note that we cannot repair phones by email and we cannot give advice about warranty claims. For warranty questions, contact your phone maker or the shop where you bought the phone.
