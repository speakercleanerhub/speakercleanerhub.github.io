Frequency Generator

Pick any frequency from 20 Hz to 20,000 Hz and play a real sine tone through your speaker. The readout always shows the exact frequency being played. Use it to find where your speaker sounds weak, buzzy or silent.

Be careful with high frequencies: tones above 10,000 Hz can be unpleasant or hard to hear, and very loud low tones can rattle a damaged speaker. Start at a low volume and raise it slowly. Do not hold the phone against your ear.

How to test your speaker with frequencies

  1. Start at 440 Hz and play the tone. It should sound smooth and clear.
  2. Move the slider down slowly toward 60 Hz. Low tones should be deep and steady. If they rattle or buzz, dust or damage may be the cause.
  3. Move the slider up toward 10,000 Hz. High tones get quieter on small phone speakers — that is normal — but they should not crackle or cut out.
  4. Note any frequency where the sound suddenly drops, buzzes or distorts. That is the range where your speaker struggles.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't I hear very high frequencies?

Most adults cannot hear above 15,000–17,000 Hz, and phone speakers are weak at those frequencies too. Hearing nothing above 16,000 Hz is normal and does not mean your speaker is broken.

What frequency is best for cleaning?

Low frequencies around 100–200 Hz move the speaker diaphragm the most, which is why the Speaker Cleaner uses 165 Hz. Use this generator to test; use the cleaner to clean.

Why does the tone change when I move the slider while playing?

The oscillator frequency updates live, so you hear the pitch change smoothly. This is a real tone being generated in your browser, not a recording.
