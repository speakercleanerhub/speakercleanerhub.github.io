Speaker Cleaner

Play real cleaning sounds through your phone speaker. Low-frequency tones vibrate the speaker diaphragm to help shake out dust and push water droplets out of the grille. Choose a mode, turn your volume up, and press Start.

Before you start: turn your phone volume up to maximum, remove the case, and hold the phone with the speaker facing down so dust and water can fall out. Do not hold the phone against your ear while the tone is playing. Sound cleaning helps with dust and water — it cannot fix a blown speaker or hardware damage.

How to use the speaker cleaner

  1. Turn your phone volume up to the highest level.
  2. Remove the phone case and hold the phone with the speaker grille facing down.
  3. Pick a mode. Low-frequency vibration works best for dust; the sweep helps with both dust and water; noise bursts are good for stubborn lint.
  4. Press Start Cleaning and let it run for the full duration. You can run it again if the sound is still muffled.
  5. After cleaning, test your speaker with the Volume Test or the Sound Test.

What each mode does

Low-frequency vibration (165 Hz): a steady sine tone at 165 Hz. Low tones move the speaker diaphragm the most, which shakes dust loose from the grille and the diaphragm.

Frequency sweep (100 Hz to 20,000 Hz): the tone rises smoothly from 100 Hz to 20,000 Hz over the selected duration, vibrating the speaker across its whole range. This reaches dust and water sitting in different parts of the grille.

Noise bursts: short bursts of sound with brief pauses. The sudden starts and stops jolt the diaphragm, which can dislodge stuck lint.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the speaker cleaner really work?

It helps with dust, lint and small amounts of water, because vibration can move those things out of the speaker grille. It does not repair hardware damage. If your speaker still sounds bad after two or three cleaning runs, read When to Visit a Repair Shop for Speaker Problems.

Is it safe for my phone?

Yes, when used as directed. The tones are ordinary sounds at normal volume. Do not hold the phone to your ear while cleaning, and stop if you hear harsh distortion — that can mean the speaker is already damaged.

How many times should I run it?

Once or twice is usually enough. If the sound is still muffled after three runs, the problem is probably a blocked grille or damaged hardware, not loose dust. Try the physical cleaning steps in How to Remove Dust from Your Phone Speaker.
