About Speaker Cleaner Hub

Speaker Cleaner Hub is a free website that helps people clean and test their phone speakers. It started from a simple, common problem: a phone that slowly gets quieter because dust, lint and pocket fluff build up in the speaker grille, or because a little water got in after rain or a splash.

Most people do not know that sound itself can help. A low-frequency tone makes the speaker diaphragm vibrate, and that vibration can shake loose dust and push small water droplets out of the grille. That is the idea behind this site: free, browser-based tools that play real cleaning and test sounds, plus plain-language guides that explain what to do — and what not to do.

What You Will Find Here

  • Speaker Cleaner tool: plays low-frequency vibration tones, a frequency sweep and noise bursts to help clear dust and water from your speaker.
  • Volume Test: plays test tones at different volume levels so you can judge whether your speaker is back to normal.
  • Frequency Generator: plays a real sine tone at any frequency from 20 Hz to 20,000 Hz.
  • Sound Test: checks left and right channels, stereo balance, bass and treble.
  • 20 detailed guides: practical, step-by-step help for iPhone and Android phones, covering dust, water, muffled sound, crackling, distortion and safe cleaning methods.

Our Approach: Honest Help

We are honest about what sound cleaning can and cannot do. It can help with dust, lint and small amounts of water. It cannot repair a blown speaker, a torn diaphragm, or a phone with water damage inside. Whenever a problem looks like hardware damage, our guides say so and point you to a repair shop instead of promising a fix. We would rather you get your phone fixed properly than keep running a tool that cannot help.

We also never ask you to download an app, create an account, or pay anything. All tools run in your browser, and nothing you do on this site is uploaded to us. For details, see our Privacy Policy.

Who This Site Is For

Anyone with a phone that sounds quiet, muffled or crackly — iPhone and Android users alike. Our guides are written in plain language, without technical jargon, so you can follow them even if you have never opened a phone or used a sound tool before.

If you have a question, a correction, or an idea for a new guide, please contact us. We read every message.
